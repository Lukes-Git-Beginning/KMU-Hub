    Create `backend/internal/biz/hr/compliance/burlg_test.go` with test cases:
    1. Full-time 5-day week, full year = 20 days minimum
    2. Full-time 5-day week, contractual 25 days = 25 days
    3. Part-time 3-day week, 20 days contract = 12 days (20 * 3/5)
    4. Part-time 4-day week, 25 days contract = 20 days (25 * 4/5)
    5. Mid-year start (July 1), 5-day week, 20 days = 10 days pro-rata (6/12 * 20)
    6. Mid-year start (March 15), full year = 15 days pro-rata (9/12 * 20, rounded to 0.5)
    7. Started on 1st of month gets that full month counted
    8. Half-day usage: 25 entitlement, 10.5 used = 14.5 remaining
    9. Carryover: 5 days carried over, before March 31 = included in remaining
    10. Carryover expired: 5 days carried over, after March 31 = NOT included
    11. First year employment (within 6 months): pro-rata 1/12 per full month worked
    12. Zero used days: remaining equals full entitlement + carryover

    Create `backend/internal/biz/hr/compliance/arbzg_test.go` with test cases:
    1. CalculateRequiredBreak: 5h work = 0 min break
    2. CalculateRequiredBreak: 6h01m work = 30 min break

    4. CalculateAutoBreakDeduction: 7h work, 30min manual break = 0 auto-deduct
    5. CalculateAutoBreakDeduction: 7h work, 20min manual break = 10 auto-deduct
    6. CalculateAutoBreakDeduction: 7h work, 0 manual break = 30 auto-deduct
    7. CalculateAutoBreakDeduction: 10h work, 30min manual break = 15 auto-deduct (needs 45)
    8. CheckWorkTime severity: 7h total = SeverityNone
    9. CheckWorkTime severity: 8h total = SeverityInfo
    10. CheckWorkTime severity: 9h total = SeverityWarning
    11. CheckWorkTime severity: 10h01m total = SeverityError
    12. CheckRestPeriod: 12h gap = no violation
    13. CheckRestPeriod: 10h gap = violation (RestViolation=true, RestHoursActual=10.0)
    14. CheckRestPeriod: nil previous end = no violation (first day)
    15. Net work time calculation: 8h clock in/out, 30min break = 450 net minutes

    **Phase GREEN: Implement to pass tests**

    Create `backend/internal/biz/hr/compliance/burlg.go`:
    - `CalculateLeaveBalance(input LeaveEntitlementInput) LeaveBalance` -- pure function implementing BUrlG rules:
      - Part-time pro-rata: annual * workDaysPerWeek / 5
      - Mid-year start: annual * remainingMonths / 12 (month counts from first full month, or start month if started on 1st)
      - First 6 months: 1/12 per full calendar month worked
      - Carryover logic: include carried_over if CalculationDate is before March 31 of current year
      - Remaining = entitlement + carryover - used (using decimal.Decimal throughout)
